Blind spot register & fabrication control

Declared uncertainties in this framework. Every control affected by a blind spot carries its BS-ID. A control with an unresolved blind spot cannot be marked [VERIFIED].

Fabrication control — source truth tags
[V] Verified — live-fetched primary source [VT] Verified-Training — confirm before client use [I] Inferred — logical derivation from verified sources [U] Unverified — structural placeholder only [FR] Fabrication-Risk — do not use without verification [BS-XX] Governed by blind spot register entry
Blind spot register — BS-01 to BS-08
BS-01
DPDP Rules not yet notified
Impact:DPDP control designs are inferred from the Act only — Rules not yet gazetted.
Mitigation: Mark all DPDP Rule references [U]. Review when Rules are notified by MeitY.
BS-02
DAKSH portal exact field schema unknown
Impact:Reporting payload templates may not match the live DAKSH portal form structure.
Mitigation: Validate against live DAKSH portal before client submission.
BS-03
ReBIT assessment framework — version currency
Impact:Framework may reference superseded assessment criteria.
Mitigation: Fetch latest from rebit.org.in before client engagement.
BS-04
NCIIPC CII financial sector designation list not fully public
Impact:Cannot confirm which entities are formally CII-designated.
Mitigation: Treat all SCBs and NBFC-UL/TL as presumptively CII-designated pending confirmation.
BS-05
RBI circulars post-August 2025 may be missing
Impact:Controls may not reflect the latest amendments or new directions.
Mitigation: Run live fetch of rbi.org.in/notifications before every engagement.
BS-06
State cooperative banks — limited coverage
Impact:Cooperative bank-specific IT governance rules are not fully mapped in this framework.
Mitigation: Exclude cooperative banks from scope or add an explicit caveat in the engagement letter.
BS-07
Foreign bank branch compliance nuances
Impact:Branch-level circular specifics may differ from subsidiary rules.
Mitigation: Flag all foreign bank controls as requiring branch-level legal review before use.
BS-08
IFTAS SFMS security standards — version unknown
Impact:INFINET security control specifications may be outdated.
Mitigation: Fetch current standards from iftas.org.in before any SFMS/INFINET audit.
